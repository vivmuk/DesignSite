ADD-ON 22 Scroll Progress Rail

Interaction / 22 · wayfinding

You always know
how far you are.

A 3px gradient rail fills from the top edge and a right-hand chapter list tracks the section in view. Both are driven by one scroll listener.

01 — Surface

Progress is reassurance.

Long pages lose people at the second fold. A visible measure of remaining distance is the cheapest retention tool on the page.

02 — Motion

The bar should never stutter.

Write scroll position in a rAF callback, not inside the event. The rail stays composited on its own layer.

03 — End note

Finish the thought.

When the bar is full, the reader is exactly where you asked them to be. That is the whole contract.
