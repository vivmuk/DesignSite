ADD-ON 04 Cursor Spotlight

Atmosphere / 04 · pointer-linked light

Light that follows attention.

A 620px radial wash tracks your pointer across the section. It is two custom properties and two gradients — and it makes a static page feel responsive to a human.

◐

One listener

A single pointermove handler writes --mx and --my on the section. Nothing else re-renders.

✳

Two temperatures

A warm orange core layered over a cool blue bloom keeps the light from looking like a flashlight.

⌁

Touch-safe

The light parks at 50% / 30% on coarse pointers, so mobile users see a composed gradient.

Move your cursor — one listener, two radial gradients, zero re-renders.
