Description

A book that happens to scroll: full-height chapters that lock into place, drop caps opening each one, marginalia in the gutters, a progress ribbon tracking reading position, and lock-and-key transitions between sections. For storytelling sites, annual letters, and founder notes where the reading IS the experience. The signature is the chapter lock — each section snaps and holds like a page turn.
