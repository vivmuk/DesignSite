Description

Each feature card pins at the top of the viewport and the next card slides up over it, while cards already stacked scale back and dim — a physical deck shuffling under the wheel. Crisp light surfaces and one confident violet keep the stack legible. Ideal for product features, pricing stories and portfolio case studies.
