Description

Ribbon-like text marquees run through every seam of the page — and they are alive: scroll faster and they accelerate, scroll back and they reverse. Outlined and solid type alternate on the belts. A streetwear energy where the page itself is the hype loop, not just the product grid.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a streetwear storefront in the Infinite Marquee Loop style. Ship one polished, self-contained page. LOOK: Poster-loud hype loop over clean retail. Palette hexes exactly: bg #f7f4ee, surface #ffffff, fg #111110, muted #6f6a62, accent #ff4fa3 (hot pink), accent2 #c6f542 (acid lime), border #e5dfd4. Fonts: load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Anton&family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;700&display=swap — Anton for every marquee belt (uppercase, letter-spacing .04em), Space Grotesk for body, captions and prices. LAYOUT, in order: (1) top bar with wordmark, four links and cart count; (2) hero with a solid black 'NEW DROP' marquee belt under the headline and a diagonal-tilted pink belt behind it; (3) product grid: three white product cards with cutout product images, mono-free price chips, hover states; (4) an opposing-direction double belt divider (pink forward, lime outlined reverse); (5) testimonial strip styled as ticker quotes; (6) about block 'Made to be worn out'; (7) mega footer with newsletter signup framed by two more belts. SIGNATURE: (a) belts between every section, alternating solid/outline; (b) visible velocity response — the page literally speeds up with you; (c) the slightly tilted belt breaking the grid. CONSTRAINTS: Build responsively with breakpoints at 768px and 1200px; keep all text at WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large) — note lime text needs dark outlines; use semantic HTML (header, nav, main, section, article, footer) with descriptive headings; lazy-load below-fold images, defer JavaScript, keep total JS under 120KB gzipped; ensure every interactive element is keyboard-reachable with a visible :focus-visible state; respect prefers-reduced-motion by pausing belts entirely.
