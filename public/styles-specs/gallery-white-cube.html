Description

The digital white cube: vast white fields where each artwork floats alone with breathing room measured in inches, labeled in tiny uppercase captions. Nothing decorates; the frame, the spacing, and the label do everything. Built for galleries and photographers whose work must remain the only object in the room.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are an exhibition designer for the web. Build a single-page gallery site in the Gallery White Cube style. LOOK: Absolute white bg #FFFFFF, off-white surface #F7F7F5, ink #111111, muted #9A9A96, border #EAEAE7, secondary gray #8A8F98, no gradients. Import: <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@300;400;500&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">. Inter at two scales only: tiny uppercase 11px letterspaced captions and large light-weight titles. LAYOUT: 1) Whisper top bar (name left, hours right). 2) First wall: one artwork centered with huge margins and floor shadow. 3) Rooms 01–04 as alternating full-width and offset walls, each one work. 4) Artist statement on a single narrow column. 5) Exhibition footer: address, hours, colophon. MOTION: IntersectionObserver triggers: artwork settles from translateY(24px) 800ms cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); captions slide 12px left-to-right 500ms after 150ms delay. Hover lifts work 4px with soft shadow, siblings dim to 0.85. Lightbox: 300ms cross-fade with 0→8px backdrop blur. All motion 200–800ms, nothing bouncy. SIGNATURE: (a) tiny caps caption format 'ARTIST — Title, Year, Medium, Dimensions', (b) numeric room markers 01/02/03 as nav, (c) hairline floor shadow under each work. CONSTRAINTS: Semantic <figure>/<figcaption>, alt text on all works, AA contrast (muted only for non-essential), responsive: margins shrink via clamp, lightbox swipeable on touch; images preloaded only for next room; prefers-reduced-motion kills transitions.
