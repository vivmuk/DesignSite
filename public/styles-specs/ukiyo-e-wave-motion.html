Description

Ukiyo-e woodblock prints in motion: Prussian-blue wave forms that actually move, mokuhanga woodgrain texture under everything, cartouche title boxes in vertical writing, and red hanko seals as stamps of authenticity. For arts, tea, and wellness brands with Japanese spirit that want the print to feel alive without becoming anime. The signature is the living wave — the hero wave undulates on a slow loop.
