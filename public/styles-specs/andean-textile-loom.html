Description

Andean weaving as layout: hand-loom band patterns as section dividers, terracotta and earth stripe palettes, chakra color sequences used honestly, tassel details on interactive elements, and woven-texture surfaces. For artisan brands, fair trade, and travel craft that want the textile to be the structure, not the decoration. The signature is the loom band — every section transition is a different woven pattern.
