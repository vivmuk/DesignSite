Description

The sign painter's craft as a website: gothic condensed lettering with hand-painted irregularity, gold-leaf effects on glass, mahl-stick drop shadows, trade banners hung between sections, and one-coat color discipline. For barbershops, breweries, and local craft where the sign IS the brand. The signature is the gold leaf — headings rendered with metallic gradient shimmer against glass.
