Description

The arcade front-end: CRT scanline bezels around every screen-like module, a pixel marquee header, INSERT COIN buttons, hiscore tables in pixel numerals, and attract-mode shimmer on idle elements. For game studios, gaming events, and pixel artists who want nostalgia with function underneath. The signature is the bezel — every content block sits inside a curved CRT frame.
