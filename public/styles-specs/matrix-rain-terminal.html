Description

The cult classic, rebuilt with restraint: phosphor-green character rain at low opacity behind a working terminal interface, katakana and Latin glyphs, CRT flicker kept subtle, and wake-up prompts as section headers. For hacker-culture brands, security firms, and privacy tools — the trick is legibility, the rain never touches the content. The signature is the wake sequence — the page boots like the movie's terminal.
