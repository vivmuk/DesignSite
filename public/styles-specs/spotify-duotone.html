Description

Every surface is washed by a duotone gradient pulled from artwork — violet-on-charcoal, green-on-black — with bold circular play buttons floating over them. Rhythm is the design system: rows, discs and equalizer bars repeat like a playlist. For music, podcast and streaming products.

MOTION: play buttons scale 0→1 with cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.4) over 200ms on card hover; equalizer bars animate height 0.8s ease-in-out infinite alternate, staggered 120ms; cards lift 4px and brighten over 250ms; header gradient fades with scroll via a throttled scroll listener. SIGNATURE: (1) per-card duotone washes pulled from artwork hues; (2) the giant circular play button; (3) the 3-bar equalizer as a living status glyph. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, focus-visible rings on all interactive rows (keyboard users navigate tracks), contrast at least 4.5:1, responsive grid 6→3→2 columns, prefers-reduced-motion stops equalizer.
