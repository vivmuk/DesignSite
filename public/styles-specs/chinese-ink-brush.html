Description

A digital handscroll: content flows horizontally like a classical shuimo painting scroll, built from layered ink washes with generous breathing room and vermilion seal-stamp punctuation. Suited to tea, culture, and art brands that want contemplative pacing. The distinctive trick is ink density doing the work of color — five gray washes and one red accent.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: Build a single-page site for a Chinese tea house in the Chinese Ink Brush style, reading like a horizontal handscroll. LOOK: background #F7F4EC, panels #FCFAF4, ink text #23272B, muted #7C7F82, single vermilion accent #B03A2E, deep slate #3B4A52, border #DAD4C4; no gradients — ink density provides all shading. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Serif+SC:wght@400;600&display=swap and use it for both display and body. LAYOUT: implement a pinned horizontal-scroll track (GSAP ScrollTrigger, xPercent -60 over 3000px of vertical scroll, scrub 1) containing sections in order: (1) opening hero with ink-wash mountain SVG, vertical headline, and seal stamp; (2) tea origin chapter with three vessel images and colophon captions; (3) brewing timeline as a brush-stroke line with nodes; (4) about chapter with a long-form paragraph in wide measure; (5) closing vermilion seal and contact line. MOTION: brush-stroke SVGs draw in with stroke-dashoffset 900ms ease-in-out; the hero wash de-blurs from 8px/0-opacity over 1.2s on load; seals press in with a 350ms spring. SIGNATURE: (a) at least one vermilion seal per chapter used as a section marker, (b) vertical colophon captions beside images, (c) wide ma negative space between sections. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, 4.5:1 contrast, provide a normal vertical-scroll fallback when JS is off or viewport is under 760px, lazy-load images, total request weight under 1.5MB.
