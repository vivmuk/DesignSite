Description

Neobrutalism with a sugar rush: 4px black borders on everything, hard offset shadows that travel when pressed, and a candy palette of bubblegum pink, grape purple and lime over cream. Zero border-radius anywhere — the honesty of brutalism with the energy of a candy store. For startups, tools and communities that want personality without pretension.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a startup/tools landing page in the Candy Neobrutalism style — brutalist structure, candy palette, tactile press physics. LOOK: pale pink #FFF1F7 background, white #FFFFFF surfaces, ink #111111 used as BOTH text and border, muted #5C5C5C, bubblegum #FF5CA8 and grape #7C6CFF as the candy pair, lime #B8F135 for highlights. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Archivo+Black&family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;700&display=swap — Archivo Black for display (uppercase), Space Grotesk for body/UI. RULES: border-radius 0 on everything, borders 4px solid #111, shadows 6-8px 8px 0 #111 (never blurred), no gradients. LAYOUT: sticky nav with bottom border 4px; hero = uppercase Archivo headline with lime highlight blocks behind key words, big purple CTA; 3-col feature grid of bordered cards with colored tag chips; a full-black stats band with lime Archivo numbers; mega footer with bordered columns. MOTION: buttons press INTO their shadow on :active — translate(6px,6px) while box-shadow collapses 6px→0 in 100ms; hover lifts translate(-4px,-4px) with shadow growing to 12px over 150ms; nav links flash lime background instantly (0ms) on hover; sections rise translateY(24px)→0 400ms ease-out staggered 80ms via IntersectionObserver. No easing curves fancier than ease-out — brutalism distrusts smoothness. SIGNATURE: (1) 4px borders + hard shadows everywhere; (2) zero radius discipline; (3) highlight blocks behind headline words; (4) press-into-shadow physics. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML; WCAG AA — #111 on #FFF1F7 passes hugely, #111 on lime and bubblegum passes for large/bold text, white text on grape passes for UI; no radius means focus outlines stay visible — keep outline-offset 2px; responsive: grid collapses to 1 col under 760px, shadows shrink to 4px; prefers-reduced-motion kills press/lift transforms.
