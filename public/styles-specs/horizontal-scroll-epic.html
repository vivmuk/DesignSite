Description

The page pins itself while the vertical wheel translates into a lateral journey through wide cinematic panels, each one a full-bleed frame with parallax layers inside. Letterboxed chrome, a panel counter and film-grade typography make it feel like a moving long take. Built for launches where the product deserves a road trip.
