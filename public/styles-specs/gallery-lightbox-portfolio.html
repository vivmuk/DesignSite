Description

The portfolio gallery perfected: masonry wall with thoughtful gap rhythm, a lightbox that opens with the clicked image as origin, filmstrip thumbnails along the lightbox bottom, exif-style captions (title, medium, year) in mono, and full keyboard navigation with arrow keys and escape. For photographers, artists, and case studies where the work must be seen large. The signature is the origin zoom — the lightbox grows from the thumbnail you clicked, not from the center.
