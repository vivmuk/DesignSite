Description

The Memphis Milano group's joyful rebellion: squiggle lines, terrazzo speckles, and confident clashes of primary red, Klein blue and bumblebee yellow on flat pastel grounds. Shapes have no respect for grid gravity — they lean, bounce and wiggle with animated SVG energy. Perfect for creative tools, kids' products and events that mistake fun for a strategy (correctly).

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a creative-tool landing page in the Memphis Milano Revival style — 1981 Memphis group energy, shapes misbehaving on a strict grid. LOOK: warm cream #FFF6E9 base, white #FFFFFF cards, near-black #2B2B2B ink AND borders (chunky 3px outlines everywhere), muted #7A6E63, primary red #FF4B3E and Klein blue #2D46FF as the clash pair, plus bumblebee #FFD23F and mint #7FDBCA as supporting splashes. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Baloo+2:wght@600;700;800&family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500;700&display=swap — Baloo 2 700–800 for display, DM Sans for body. No gradients — flat color only, Memphis never gradiented. LAYOUT: nav with chunky-outlined logo and a red blocky CTA; hero: big Baloo headline with an SVG squiggle underline, geometric shapes (triangle/circle/zigzag/dot cluster) scattered at ±15deg angles around it; feature cards in a 3-col grid with 3px black borders, hard 8px offset shadows, terrazzo-speckle panel headers (CSS multiple radial-gradient dots); an events/workshop strip as tilted tickets; footer with blob-character marquee. MOTION: squiggle underlines draw via stroke-dashoffset 100%→0 over 600ms ease-out on scroll into view; decorative shapes wiggle rotate ±10deg with a squish on 4s ease-in-out infinite, phase-offset per shape; cards bounce translateY(-8px) 350ms cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) on hover with speckle scatter (background-position jitter); footer blobs hop a marquee 8s linear with 360° rotation per hop. Vanilla JS only for the squiggle observer. SIGNATURE: (1) squiggle underlines that draw themselves; (2) terrazzo speckle fills; (3) everything outlined in 3px black with offset shadows; (4) deliberately unaligned decorative shapes. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML; WCAG AA — #7A6E63 on cream for body, shapes always decorative (aria-hidden); cards remain readable when tilted (≤1deg tilt on interactive elements); responsive: shapes reduce in count and size under 800px, grid single column under 640px; prefers-reduced-motion freezes wiggles and draws squiggles instantly; inline SVG only, no icon fonts; total JS < 8KB.
