Description

Scrolling is propulsion: a full-screen raymarched shader tunnel — rings, struts and light streaks — flies the visitor through hyperspace toward the product. Content appears as station signage between velocity segments. For games, web3 launches and event reveals.

LOOK: void #030308 base with a #14062B depth gradient; panels #101021, borders #232346; text #F0EFFF, muted #8282B8; tunnel geometry glows hot pink #FF3D68 with teal #38F2C8 counter-glow. LAYOUT: full-viewport shader hero rendering an SDF tunnel (rings + longitudinal struts) flying toward the camera; a speed HUD readout bottom-right (in mono); between velocity segments, 'docking stations' — solid panels that scale from .92 to 1 when the tunnel decelerates; content order: hero title over the tunnel, three station sections (story, specs, access), final CTA that triggers warp. MOTION: scroll delta drives a z-velocity uniform (damped 0.9 per frame); rings brighten with proximity via glow falloff in the shader; when velocity ≈ 0 for 400ms, the nearest station docks into view (400ms scale+fade); CTA click fires warp — velocity ×2 and FOV widens 60→75 for 1.5s. SIGNATURE: (1) scroll literally propels you — stop and you hover; (2) station-dock reveal rhythm; (3) the warp burst on CTA. CONSTRAINTS: single fullscreen quad shader (no geometry), DPR cap 1.25 (raymarching is expensive), throttle to 30fps on integrated GPUs via adaptive resolution, static poster + normal scroll fallback for reduced-motion/no-WebGL, semantic HTML over the canvas.
