Description

The 19th-century statistical atlas reborn: engraved-style choropleths with hatching, legend keys drawn as plates, population pyramids, and folio numbering as navigation. For open data, urban planning, and civic tech that wants authority and charm in equal measure. The signature is the hatched choropleth — regions filled with engraved line patterns instead of flat color.
