Description

Aged-paper backgrounds carry engraved-style ornaments, copperplate flourishes, dense almanac tables, and a deep oxblood accent — a page that smells of cedar and wax seals. Use it for whiskey, fine publishing, and boutiques. Distinctive because every rule and border appears to have been etched by hand.
