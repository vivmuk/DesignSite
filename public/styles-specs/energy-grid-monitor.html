Description

A control-room page: single-line diagrams of the grid with animated flow direction, megawatt counters rolling, and alarm states painted in unambiguous red/amber/green. Dense panels, engineering labels, ISA-101 restraint. For utilities and industrial dashboards where misreading a state is not an option.
