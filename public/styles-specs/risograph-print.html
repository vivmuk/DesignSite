Description

The risograph press as an interface: two fluorescent spot inks (pink and blue) that misregister on purpose, halftone dot fills, overprint multiply blends where inks overlap, and paper grain over everything. Typography reads like poster proofs, sections feel like stacked prints pulled by hand. For studios, publishers and events that want warmth, texture and print-shop credibility.
