Description

Kente-woven stripes run through a deep forest-and-gold palette, giving every section a proud geometric rhythm of repeated bands and counters. Built for culture, fashion, and finance brands celebrating West African heritage with modern confidence. The signature is the woven band system — headers, dividers, and stats all carry the same stripe language.
