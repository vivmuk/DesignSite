Description

Danish living-room comfort: pale wood tones, oat and moss accents, wool-grain textures, and photography bathed in soft window daylight. For furniture, lifestyle, and wellness brands that sell calm. The distinctive move is tactility — everything from shadows to buttons feels like wool, oak, and matte ceramic.

ROLE: Build a single-page site for a Scandinavian furniture brand in the Scandinavian Hygge style. LOOK: background #F4F1EB with a gentle top-to-bottom gradient, panels #FDFBF7, text #2F2C28, muted #8B8378, accents #B5824C (oak) and #6B7F72 (moss), border #E2DACE. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,400;9..144,600&family=Karla:wght@400;500;700&display=swap — Fraunces for display, Karla for body. LAYOUT in order: (1) header with wordmark and three links; (2) hero: split layout, headline left, sunlit product photo right, single CTA; (3) product grid of three white cards with soft wool shadows; (4) oak-swatch divider strip; (5) about section on craft and materials; (6) testimonial quote; (7) moss-green CTA band; (8) minimal footer. MOTION: sections fade in from opacity 0.4 over 700ms with no movement (light arriving); card shadows breathe via 5s ease-in-out keyframes; buttons depress 2px over 120ms on active; a wood-grain background layer parallaxes at 0.3x scroll rate. SIGNATURE: (a) wool-texture card shadows that visibly breathe, (b) pale-wood swatch divider strips, (c) extremely generous whitespace (96px+ section padding). CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, 4.5:1 contrast, single column below 760px, no JS libraries beyond a tiny rAF parallax, images lazy-loaded. DELIVERY: Ship one self-contained index.html. Define CSS custom properties --bg:#F4F1EB, --surface:#FDFBF7, --fg:#2F2C28, --muted:#8B8378, --accent:#B5824C, --accent2:#6B7F72, --border:#E2DACE on :root and use them everywhere — no hard-coded colors. Preconnect to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, load Fraunces, Karla with display=swap and a system-font fallback stack to prevent layout shift. Implement the motions (daylight fade, wool shadow breathe, soft press, slow parallax) exactly as specified; wrap every ambient loop in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to disable. Verify before delivery: all headings in a logical h1→h3 order, focus-visible outlines on interactive elements, no horizontal overflow at 360px viewport, and images with width/height attributes to avoid CLS.
