Description

A contract becomes a webpage: numbered clauses with hanging indents, bookish serif body at reading measure, and margin annotations in small gray notes. Ink black on paper white with oxblood accents for defined terms. Cross-references link like case citations. Authority through typography, not chrome.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a legal-publishing web typesetter. Build a single-page terms/contract site in the Legal Document Serif style. LOOK: Paper bg #FDFBF6, white surface #FFFFFF, ink #1F1B16, muted #7A7268, oxblood accent #7A2E2B, slate blue #2F4858, border #E2DACC, no gradients. Import: <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=EB+Garamond:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;1,400&family=Courier+Prime:wght@400&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">. EB Garamond everywhere textual, Courier Prime for annotations and dates. LAYOUT: 1) Masthead with double rule. 2) Preamble. 3) Articles as numbered clauses (CSS counters: 1 / 1.1 / 1.1.2) with hanging indents at 68ch measure. 4) Margin annotations via a two-column grid on wide screens. 5) Definitions section with oxblood small-caps terms. 6) Signature blocks with dotted lines. 7) Footer with drawn wax seal. MOTION: Clauses rise 12px + fade 500ms ease-out staggered 60ms (IntersectionObserver). Margin notes slide in 300ms when their clause centers. Defined-term hover highlights all references 400ms via JS class toggle. Seal draws 2s stroke animation. SIGNATURE: (a) CSS-counter clause numbering with hanging indents, (b) margin-note grid with connecting hairlines, (c) oxblood defined-term linking. CONSTRAINTS: Semantic <ol>/<section> structure, print stylesheet (@media print: hide motion, black on white), AA contrast, responsive: margin notes collapse to inline footnotes under 960px; anchor links for cross-references with :target highlight; no JS frameworks needed. EXTRA: Include Schedule A/B stub anchors at the end; clause hover shows a subtle left-rule marker; document title in the browser tab matches the masthead; keep line length 65-70ch measured in characters.
