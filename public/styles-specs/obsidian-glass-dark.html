Description

Deep-black canvas with translucent frosted panels stacked at different blur depths, each rimmed with a faint blue glow. A cursor spotlight reveals light beneath the glass as you move. Premium and calm — made for fintech dashboards, desktop apps and flagship SaaS.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a fintech dashboard landing page in the Obsidian Glass Dark style — layered frosted glass over glowing black, calm and expensive-feeling. LOOK: near-black #06070B background with a radial #6C8CFF ambient light bleeding from the top edge; text #E8ECF7, muted #7C86A3, borders #232A3D. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter+Tight:wght@600;700&family=Inter:wght@400;500&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;600&display=swap. Glass panels use background rgba(16,19,28,.55), backdrop-filter blur(20px) saturate(140%), 1px borders with a top-edge highlight and a faint #6C8CFF glow rim (box-shadow inset + outer). A soft cursor spotlight (radial gradient, about 10% alpha, 400px) lerps after the pointer. LAYOUT: floating pill nav; hero with headline left and one large glass KPI panel right; stats band of four glass tiles; chart-suite section with layered glass charts at three blur planes; settings-panel-style feature list; pricing as three glass tiers with the middle one lifted and brighter; footer as a thin glass strip. MOTION: spotlight follows the pointer with an 80ms lerp via requestAnimationFrame; panels tilt 2deg toward the cursor (perspective 800px, 300ms ease-out); panels fade-up 20px with 80ms stagger on entry; KPI digits tick up 1.4s cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). SIGNATURE: (1) visible blur-depth layering between panels; (2) glow rims on every glass edge; (3) the ambient top light. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, text contrast at least 4.5:1 over glass, provide -webkit-backdrop-filter fallback and a solid-color fallback, responsive stacks, prefers-reduced-motion disables tilt and spotlight.
