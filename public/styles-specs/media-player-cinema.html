Description

A cinema-grade player kit: wide scrub rails with chapter thumbnails, hover previews, ambient light bleeding from the playing frame, and timecode-precise controls. For streaming, courses, and podcasts where the player is the product. The signature is environmental light — the room glows with whatever is on screen.

ROLE: Build a single-page streaming interface in the Media Player Cinema style. LOOK: background #0B0D12 with a radial wash, panels #14171F, text #EDEFF4, muted #8A90A0, accents #FF4D5A (signal red) and #FFC24B (amber), border #232838. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@500;700&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@500&display=swap — Space Grotesk display, Inter body, JetBrains Mono timecode. LAYOUT: (1) minimal header with logo and search; (2) hero player: 16:9 frame with poster frame, center play control, and an ambient blurred glow layer behind it; (3) scrub rail with buffered range, chapter tick markers, and hover thumbnail tooltip; (4) control cluster — play, skip, volume, speed, PiP, fullscreen with mono timecode; (5) chapter sidebar with mini-thumb list; (6) related row of four episode cards; (7) footer. MOTION: the ambient glow is a blurred duplicate frame whose hue shifts every 6s ease-in-out; scrub-hover shows a thumbnail tooltip lerping at 80ms; chapter markers stagger in 60ms apart over 400ms; controls fade out after 3s idle and return instantly on pointer move. SIGNATURE: (a) ambient screen light on the room, (b) the chapter-tick scrub rail with preview, (c) mono timecodes. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML with aria-valuenow on the rail, keyboard space/arrow control, 4.5:1 contrast on overlays, single column below 760px, no external player libraries. DELIVERY: Ship one self-contained index.html. Define CSS custom properties --bg:#0B0D12, --surface:#14171F, --fg:#EDEFF4, --muted:#8A90A0, --accent:#FF4D5A, --accent2:#FFC24B, --border:#232838 on :root and use them everywhere — no hard-coded colors. Preconnect to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, load Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono with display=swap and a system-font fallback stack to prevent layout shift. Implement the motions (ambient glow, scrub preview, chapter sweep, control fade) exactly as specified; wrap every ambient loop in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to disable. Verify before delivery: all headings in a logical h1→h3 order, focus-visible outlines on interactive elements, no horizontal overflow at 360px viewport, and images with width/height attributes to avoid CLS.
