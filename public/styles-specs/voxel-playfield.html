Description

A voxel world as interface: isometric dioramas built from chunky blocks, props that pop with bounce easing, a day-night cycle tinting the whole scene, and blocky particles on every interaction. For games, web toys, and kids' tech where the aesthetic should feel playable. The signature is the diorama case — each section lives inside its own little world under glass.
