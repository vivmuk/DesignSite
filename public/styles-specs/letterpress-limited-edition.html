Description

Deep letterpress impression translated to screen: cotton-paper whites, ink that sits IN the page rather than on it, one or two spot colors (indigo and fire-engine red), hand-set vernacular with ornaments, and deckled edges. For print studios, small-batch brands, letterpress shops, and invitations. The signature is the deboss — type rendered with inner shadows that read as physically pressed.
