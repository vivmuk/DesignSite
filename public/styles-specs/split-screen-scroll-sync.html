Description

The viewport splits down a center rule: the left column scrolls down while the right scrolls up, images counter-flowing past each other with captions locked to each plate. Gallery-calm palette and high-contrast serif captions. A fashion-editorial device that turns comparison and sequence into one elegant contradiction.
