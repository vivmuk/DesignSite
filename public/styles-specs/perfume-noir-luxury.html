Description

A near-black fragrance page where a single warm spotlight follows the cursor over macro product photography. Copy is minimal, set in delicate serif italics, letting the bottle and the darkness do the selling. Distinctive for its restraint: one accent hue, one product, infinite atmosphere.

LOOK: Background #0B0908, surface #161211, ivory text #F4EDE3, muted #7A6F65, amber accent #D99A3D, deep amber #8C5A2B, borders #2A2320, page wash `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 20%, #2A1F14, #0B0908 65%)`. Import: <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,300;0,400;0,600;1,300;1,400&family=EB+Garamond:ital@0;1&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">. Cormorant Garamond for display, EB Garamond italic for whisper copy. LAYOUT: 1) Minimal letter-spaced nav. 2) Full-viewport hero: centered bottle in an amber spotlight, one-line tagline. 3) Scent-note pyramid section (top/heart/base) with hairline dividers. 4) Full-bleed atmospheric photography strip. 5) Single testimonial in italic. 6) Quiet footer with boutique addresses. MOTION: Cursor spotlight = 600px radial gradient lerping to pointer at 0.08 ease, mix-blend-mode screen, rAF-driven. Bottle floats ±10px over a 6s ease-in-out loop. Note items fade up 400ms staggered 200ms on intersection. Smoke layer parallaxes at 0.4x scroll. SIGNATURE: (a) the amber cursor spotlight, (b) scent-note pyramid with hairline connectors, (c) whisper copy in italic serif at 2rem and 60% opacity. CONSTRAINTS: Semantic HTML5 landmarks, WCAG AA — keep body text ≥ #B8AC9E equivalent on black; prefers-reduced-motion disables float/spotlight drift; responsive: hero stacks, spotlight becomes a static vignette on touch; images use srcset; no CLS.
