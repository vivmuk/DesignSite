Description

Taisho-era Tokyo modernism: warm andon-lantern cream lit by vermilion and pine-green, asanoha and seigaiha lattice patterns edging every panel, and slender vertical rule lines that organize content like hanging scrolls. Use it for restaurants, craft houses, and publishers who want heritage without stuffiness. The distinctive move is East-meets-West geometry — deco symmetry drawn with Japanese pattern vocabulary.
