Description

Profound whitespace, a single sumi-ink headline, one vermillion seal accent, and vertical Japanese captions floating at the margin. Use it for architecture studios, tea brands, and quiet portfolios. Distinctive because the design communicates through what is withheld — Ma, the interval, is the interface.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: Build an architecture studio portfolio in the Japanese Minimal Ma style — negative space as the primary material. LOOK: Palette — bg #FBFAF6, surface #FFFFFF, fg #1C1C1C, muted #9B968C, accent #B3402E (vermillion), accent2 #5B6E63 (moss), border #E7E3DA, no gradient. Load fonts via https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Shippori+Mincho:wght@400;500;600&family=Zen+Kaku+Gothic+New:wght@300;400;500&display=swap — Shippori Mincho display, Zen Kaku Gothic New body. Keep 60% or more of every viewport empty; content max-width 34em; no boxes, few borders. LAYOUT: (1) nav — no bar, four small links top-right and a wordmark top-left; (2) hero — one Mincho sentence placed low-left, vertical tategaki caption on the right margin, vermillion seal below; (3) philosophy section with a slowly drawn enso circle; (4) works list — text-only index rows (project, year, location) with hairline rules; (5) image-lightbox gallery — full-bleed single photos with generous margins; (6) contact form-stack (three fields, hairline underlines, no boxes); (7) footer-mega reduced to three lines. MOTION: SVG stroke-draw — brush rules and the enso animate stroke-dashoffset over 1.4-1.8s ease-in-out via IntersectionObserver; text fades up 10px over 1.2s with 300ms delay; vertical captions slide down 12px once; nothing loops, nothing bounces. SIGNATURE: The self-drawing enso; the vermillion seal as the only saturated color on the page; vertical text accents in writing-mode: vertical-rl. CONSTRAINTS: Semantic HTML, lang attributes for Japanese text, focus states visible on hairline inputs, single column under 720px, images lazy-loaded with width and height set, page weight under 200KB.
