Description

A fixed progress rail conducts the whole page: chapter numbers tick over, accent color warms from teal to amber, and stats count up as their chapter arrives. Bookish serif over a calm sans keeps it course-and-report serious. For long documents that deserve a sense of journey rather than a wall of text.
