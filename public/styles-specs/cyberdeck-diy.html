Description

A lovingly soldered mess: amber OLED readouts bolted onto brushed-metal surfaces, cable runs, masking-tape labels and stenciled warnings. Every panel feels built in a garage at 2 a.m. — and that is the point. For hardware companies, maker communities and indie games.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a hardware startup's site in the Cyberdeck DIY style — the page must look hand-assembled from salvage, yet stay usable. LOOK: near-black #0E0C08 background, amber #FFC46B OLED text, hot #FF9E1F accents, tape-cream #E8DCC8 labels, borders #35291A. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Chakra+Petch:wght@600;700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700&display=swap — Chakra Petch for headers, Space Mono everywhere. Panels sit on #1A160E metal surfaces with screw-head dots in corners (radial-gradient pseudo-elements) and vent-slat textures. LAYOUT: top rail styled as a power switch + nav; hero as the deck's main OLED screen with a boot self-test readout; feature grid as bolted modules; product cards with serial-number stamps and stenciled CAUTION labels; settings-panel-style comparison table; CTA as a big red-guard switch; mega footer with a 'schematics' sitemap. MOTION: OLED panels power on with a 3-step 600ms flicker; modules slide in with cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.2) servo overshoot; LEDs blink staggered 2s steps(1); hover draws a skewed masking-tape underline over 240ms. SIGNATURE: (1) screw and vent greebles on every panel corner; (2) masking-tape labels at slight rotations; (3) serial-number stamps (DECK-042 / REV C). CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, amber-on-dark text at least 4.5:1 contrast, single-column mobile layout, prefers-reduced-motion kills flicker, textures must be pure CSS — no image assets. Ensure the boot self-test readout in the hero displays believable diagnostics that update on each visit. Keep every panel titled like workshop documentation — 'MODULE', 'BATCH', 'REV' — so the whole page reads as one continuous bench build.
