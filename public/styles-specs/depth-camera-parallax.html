Description

Photography goes 2.5D: each hero image is sliced into depth planes that separate under cursor and scroll movement, creating a window-into-the-scene effect. Type floats between layers at its own depth. For travel, photography portfolios and film promotion.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a travel-photography site in the Depth-Camera Parallax style — photographs split into 2.5D layers that respond to cursor and scroll like a window into the scene. LOOK: cinematic dark #0C0F14 base, panels #141922, borders #222A38; text #F2F5F9, muted #8B96A5; warm #FFD166 sunrise accents and cool #5FA8D3 shadow accents. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,500;9..144,700&family=Karla:wght@400;500&display=swap — Fraunces for display with its expressive optical axis, Karla for captions and body. LAYOUT: letterboxed hero: a mountain-valley photo sliced into 4 stacked planes (foreground rocks, mid trees, far ridges, sky) using absolutely positioned layers with translateZ, pointer parallax moving each at depth-scaled factors (24px/12px/6px/3px); a location caption floats in front at 1.4× factor; gallery of 6 thumbs below, each splitting into 2 layers on hover; image-lightbox opens full-bleed with the deepest parallax; journal/testimonial section; minimal nav. MOTION: pointer movement lerps layer offsets over 120ms via rAF; scrolling the hero increases separation from 0 to 40px across 150vh; active layer breathes brightness ±2% over 3s; captions drift at 1.4× for a floating feel. SIGNATURE: (1) visible layer separation — you can see the photo's bones; (2) captions floating IN FRONT of the scene; (3) letterbox bars framing every frame. CONSTRAINTS: layers are pre-sliced static images (or CSS gradients for the demo) — no runtime depth maps needed; pointer parallax disabled on touch (use device-orientation or scroll only); provide flat fallback for prefers-reduced-motion; lazy-load gallery; contrast at least 4.5:1.
