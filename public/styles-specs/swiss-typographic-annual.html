Description

The Swiss annual report at its most refined: a strict baseline grid, folio lines top and bottom, footnotes and source citations everywhere, tabular lining figures in tables, and one red used only for statistics. For cultural institutions, foundations, and reports that want authority through typography rather than imagery. Distinctive because the page reads like a printed document that happens to be on screen.
