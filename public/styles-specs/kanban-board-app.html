Description

A Trello-school productivity kit: draggable cards with springy drop physics, column headers with WIP counters, avatar clusters, labels, and checklists. For productivity and project-management products that live or die by drag feel. The signature is tactile honesty — cards lift with shadow, tilt slightly, and settle with a spring.

ROLE: Build a single-page kanban board in the Kanban Board App style. LOOK: background #F1F3F7 with a subtle gradient, white cards and columns, text #20242E, muted #7A8296, accents #0079BF (board blue) and #61BD4F (done green), border #DFE3EC. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@500&display=swap — Inter for all UI, mono for WIP counts. LAYOUT: (1) board header with project title, member avatar cluster, and filter bar; (2) four columns — Backlog, In progress (WIP 4), Review, Done — each with a count chip, scrollable card stack, and an add-card ghost row; (3) cards carry a colored label ribbon, title, checklist tick count, due badge, and avatar footer; (4) card modal opens on click with details and activity; (5) footer hint bar with keyboard shortcuts. MOTION: dragged card lifts with scale 1.03, ±2deg tilt, deepened shadow over 150ms; valid drop columns brighten their border to blue in 180ms; on drop, neighbors animate apart with 320ms spring cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1); checking a checklist item strikes text over 250ms with a green pop. SIGNATURE: (a) WIP count chips in every header, (b) tactile lift-tilt-shadow drag feel, (c) add-card ghost rows. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML with lists, full keyboard DnD fallback (move buttons), 4.5:1 contrast, responsive columns scroll horizontally below 800px, no frameworks.
