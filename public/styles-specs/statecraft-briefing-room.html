Description

The situation room as a website: a world map with dossier pins, flag chips for country entities, classification stamps, and a cable telex feed streaming diplomatic traffic in mono. For think tanks, geopolitical analysis, and intelligence-adjacent products that want gravitas with density. The signature is the cable — analysis arrives as telex-style dispatches, timestamped and numbered.
