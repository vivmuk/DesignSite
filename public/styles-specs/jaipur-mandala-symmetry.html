Description

Radial symmetry as interface: content arranged around mandala motifs, marigold-and-rose palette, and jali-lattice screens filtering light across sections. Built for weddings, jewelry, and hospitality that want ceremony and warmth. The signature is a centered, mirrored composition where ornament frames — never crowds — the message.

ROLE: Build a single-page site for a Jaipur wedding-planning studio in the Jaipur Mandala Symmetry style. LOOK: background #FFF6EC with a soft radial marigold glow, panels #FFFDF8, text #4A2333, muted #A07868, accents #E8871E (marigold) and #C23B67 (rose), border #F0DCC4. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Marcellus&family=Karla:wght@400;500;700&display=swap — Marcellus for display, Karla for body. LAYOUT in order: (1) header with centered monogram-style brand; (2) fully centered hero: a large mandala SVG behind a two-line mirrored Marcellus headline, one paragraph, two ornament buttons; (3) three ceremony cards under a marigold garland strip; (4) symmetric testimonial wall (two columns mirroring each other); (5) pricing tiers as three tiered arch cards; (6) CTA band with lattice overlay; (7) footer with corner flourishes. MOTION: the hero mandala rotates from -90deg and scales 0.8→1 over 1.1s ease-out; mirrored content pairs slide in from ±24px over 600ms; a jali-lattice background layer drifts 40px over 12s on a loop; button hovers bloom a gold ring shadow over 250ms. SIGNATURE: (a) every section anchored by a radial mandala motif, (b) jali lattice dividers, (c) strict left-right mirrored composition. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML, 4.5:1 contrast on body text, single column below 760px with mandala scaled down, CSS-first motion, lazy-load photography. DELIVERY: Ship one self-contained index.html. Define CSS custom properties --bg:#FFF6EC, --surface:#FFFDF8, --fg:#4A2333, --muted:#A07868, --accent:#E8871E, --accent2:#C23B67, --border:#F0DCC4 on :root and use them everywhere — no hard-coded colors. Preconnect to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, load Marcellus, Karla with display=swap and a system-font fallback stack to prevent layout shift. Implement the motions (mandala bloom, jali light sweep, symmetry unfold, petal hover) exactly as specified; wrap every ambient loop in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to disable. Verify before delivery: all headings in a logical h1→h3 order, focus-visible outlines on interactive elements, no horizontal overflow at 360px viewport, and images with width/height attributes to avoid CLS.
