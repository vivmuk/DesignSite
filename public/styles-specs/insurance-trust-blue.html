Description

Insurance built to lower heart rates: a steady mid-blue, shield and umbrella iconography, and claim flows broken into reassuring steps with progress you can see. Rounded but serious, generous with explanations, allergic to dark patterns. Every screen says: you're covered, and here's exactly what happens next.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a trust-first product designer. Build a single-page insurance site in the Insurance Trust Blue style. LOOK: Soft blue-white bg #F4F8FB, white surface #FFFFFF, deep navy ink #17324A, muted #5B7893, trust blue #1668B0, confirm green #2E9E6B, border #D5E3EE, gradient `linear-gradient(180deg,#F4F8FB,#E9F2F9)`. Import: <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Public+Sans:wght@400;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">. Public Sans only — legibility over personality. LAYOUT: 1) Nav with quote CTA. 2) Hero: shield badge with pulse ring + headline + GET A QUOTE. 3) Claim flow stepper: File → Assess → Resolve with progress rail. 4) Coverage comparison table (checkmark columns). 5) Stats band: claims paid, avg response, years. 6) Plain-language FAQ accordion. 7) Trust footer with licenses. MOTION: Progress rail fills 400ms ease-out per step, check pops scale 200ms. Shield badges scale 0.92→1 250ms. One teal ring pulse 600ms on load. Premium quote counts up $0→value 900ms ease-out. SIGNATURE: (a) always-visible claim progress rail, (b) shield/umbrella badge system, (c) 'what happens next' explainer boxes after each step. CONSTRAINTS: WCAG 2.2 AA, forms with visible labels and inline errors (no alert()), stepper semantic <ol>, AA contrast everywhere, responsive: table becomes stacked cards under 720px; no countdowns/urgency patterns; reduced-motion disables pulse; page works without JS (static flow shown). EXTRA: Include a coverage-calc teaser input (zip + age) that scrolls to the quote form; every step box repeats the phone number as an alternative path; use sentence case throughout, never shouting.
