Description

A cyclorama-white product lab where the hero is a rotating turntable stage ringed with degree ticks, lit by a sweeping studio highlight, and driven by material-swap chips and an exploded-view toggle. Built for hardware launches and industrial design studios that want the object treated like evidence. The signature is instrumentation: every claim about the product is pinned to it with dimension callouts, mono spec readouts, and leader lines instead of marketing prose.
