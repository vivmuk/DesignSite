Description

A Cal.com-school scheduling kit: month grids with availability dots, timezone selector chips, bookable slot chips that snap on hover, and a booking summary drawer. For scheduling, health, and salon flows where choosing a time is the whole product. The signature is effortless slot selection — hover states, keyboard arrows, and instant confirm.

ROLE: Build a single-page scheduling product page in the Calendar Booking Suite style. LOOK: background #FCFCFD with a faint gradient, white panels, text #1B1F27, muted #767E8C, accents #4F46E5 (indigo) and #F59E0B (amber for pending), border #E8EAF1. Load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@500&display=swap — Jakarta Sans throughout, mono for time numerals. LAYOUT in order: (1) header with brand and a booking-link pill; (2) booking hero: host avatar pill with name, duration, and description; (3) two-panel workspace — left month calendar grid with availability density dots and prev/next controls, right two columns of bookable slot chips grouped morning/afternoon; (4) selected-slot summary drawer with confirm button and add-to-calendar row; (5) FAQ strip; (6) footer. MOTION: slot chips lift 2px and fill indigo on hover over 140ms, locking with a 200ms pop when selected; months crossfade with 24px slide over 260ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1); today's cell pulses a ring every 3s; the drawer rises with 320ms cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). SIGNATURE: (a) availability dots inside calendar cells, (b) timezone chip row, (c) the rising confirm drawer. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML (button elements for slots), arrow-key calendar navigation, aria-selected states, 4.5:1 contrast, single column below 720px, no frameworks. DELIVERY: Ship one self-contained index.html. Preconnect to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, load Plus Jakarta Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono with display=swap and a system-font fallback stack to prevent layout shift. Implement the motions (slot snap, month slide, day pulse, drawer rise) exactly as specified; wrap every ambient loop in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to disable.
