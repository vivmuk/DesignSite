Description

A real-time 3D product stage takes center screen: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, swap materials and colors live under studio lighting. Supporting content stays minimal so the object remains the star. For hardware, sneakers and furniture brands selling craft through inspection.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a product page in the Three.js Product Viewer style — a real-time 3D stage where the customer inspects the object like it is in their hands. LOOK: warm paper-white #F4F2EE background, #FFFFFF surfaces, #1A1A1A text, #8A8578 muted, #E2DFD8 borders, orange #FF5A1F accent for selection states. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@500;700&family=Inter:wght@400;500&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400&display=swap. The 3D viewport is full-bleed on desktop (60vh) with a soft radial floor shadow baked under the model. LAYOUT: slim top nav; hero = 3D viewport occupying most of the viewport with headline overlaid top-left and a vertical swatch rail right (5 colors, active one ringed in #FF5A1F); annotation hotspots on the model opening small spec cards; below, a tab-system (Specs / Materials / Care) with real content; three product-card recommendations; CTA add-to-cart bar that stays sticky on scroll. MOTION: three.js OrbitControls with enableDamping 0.08 and autoRotate 0.6 until first pointerdown; model entrance via GSAP from y+1.2 with elastic.out(1, 0.6) over 1.2s; swatch clicks tween material.color over 300ms ease-out plus a 500ms key-light intensity pulse; hotspots pulse scale 1→1.15 every 2s. SIGNATURE: (1) the always-grabbable model — cursor becomes grab/grabbing; (2) swatch rail with live material tween; (3) hotspot annotations tied to spec data. CONSTRAINTS: load GLTF with Draco compression, show a progress bar during load and a static fallback image if WebGL fails, cap pixelRatio at 2, pause rendering when tab hidden (visibilitychange), semantic HTML, contrast ≥4.5:1.
