Description

The engineer's drafting table as a website: cyanotype blue field, white line drawings, dimension lines with arrows, title blocks in the corner, revision stamps, and monospace annotations everywhere. For architecture firms, fabrication studios, and makers who want the page to read like a drawing set. The signature is the dimension line — every element measured and labeled.
