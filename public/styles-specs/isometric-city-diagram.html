Description

Infrastructure rendered as a living isometric metropolis: districts are product areas, roads are data flows and animated packets travel the streets. Tilt, pan and click a district to zoom into real documentation. Playful precision for infra, logistics and crypto platforms.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building an infrastructure platform page in the Isometric City Diagram style — the product shown as a living isometric metropolis where districts are features and roads carry animated packets. LOOK: slate-navy #101623 background, blocks on #1A2233 with #242E44 edges; text #E9EFF9, muted #6E7C96; amber #FFB03A for the compute district and highlights, green #4FD08C for the healthy/status district. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Outfit:wght@400;500;600&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400&display=swap and a rounded display face (Chillax, or fallback Bricolage Grotesque). The isometric plane uses a 30° grid: each district is an extruded block (CSS transform: rotateX(60deg) rotateZ(45deg) on a parent, or an orthographic three.js scene). LAYOUT: nav with a tiny live status dot; hero headline above a large isometric cityscape — districts labeled Compute, Storage, Edge, Observability, Payments; roads between them carry SVG dashed packet trails animating continuously; hovering a district lifts it 6px and shows a stats chip; clicking deep-links to that feature's docs section; below, a stats band and data table of regional latency; footer with a minimap-style sitemap. MOTION: packets animate via stroke-dashoffset 2.4s linear infinite; districts rise in a load sequence with 60ms stagger and a spring overshoot; hovered district extrudes with 200ms ease-out; the whole plane drifts ±2° over 30s alternate for ambient life. SIGNATURE: (1) animated packet trails along the roads; (2) hover-lift + label chips on districts; (3) the corner minimap tracking viewport. CONSTRAINTS: prefers-reduced-motion shows a static labeled diagram; keyboard users get a district list mirroring the map as real links; contrast at least 4.5:1; keep the CSS-3D version under 30KB JS.
