Description

A hundred-year-old house translated to the web: repeating monogram patterns, crest motifs, serif small-caps, and engraving-grade formality. Deep green and ivory with gold rules, like a private bank's lobby. Speaks in founding dates and numbered editions; trust is inherited, not claimed.

LOOK: Ivory bg #F4F0E6, warm surface #FBF8F0, forest ink #232A22, muted #7C7A6C, antique gold #8A6D2F, heritage green #2E4434, border #DDD4BE, no gradients. Import: <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+SC:wght@400;500;600&family=Lora:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;1,400&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">. Cormorant SC small caps for display, Lora for body. LAYOUT: 1) Crest medallion header + small-caps nav. 2) Hero with founding-date statement and gold rule. 3) Heritage timeline (engraved nodes). 4) Collection cards with certificate-style double borders and № numbering. 5) Stats band (years, ateliers, editions). 6) Quiet CTA. 7) Footer with maison addresses and small-caps columns. MOTION: Crest embosses in (opacity + scale 0.97→1, 900ms ease-out). Monogram tile background parallaxes at 0.2x scroll via transform. Edition numbers count up 1s ease-out. SIGNATURE: (a) repeating monogram tile at ~4% opacity, (b) certificate-style double-rule borders on cards, (c) '№' edition numbering and founding date as recurring motifs. CONSTRAINTS: Semantic HTML, AA contrast, responsive: monogram pattern via SVG data-URI, cards stack under 720px; count-up respects prefers-reduced-motion (jump to final value); fonts subset weights; no third-party trackers. EXTRA: Generate the monogram tile as an SVG data-URI; add a subtle paper-grain overlay at 2% opacity; edition numbers must be roman-free arabic; keep all rules 1px; preload the display font only.
