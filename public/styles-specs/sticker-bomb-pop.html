Description

Every element behaves like a die-cut sticker: thick white kiss-cut borders, black outlines, hard drop shadows, and layouts built from overlapping rotated stickers instead of flat blocks. Elements drop in with spring overshoot, peel at the corners on hover, and hard-invert like a flash print. For youth brands, games and merch drops where the page should feel like a sticker sheet come alive.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a merch/sticker shop in the Sticker Bomb Pop style — a sticker sheet come alive, playful physics, thick outlines, fully shoppable underneath. LOOK: pale banana-cream #FFF6D6 background, white #FFFFFF sticker surfaces, near-black #17171B ink AND borders, muted #716C63, hot pink #FF4D6D and sky teal #4CC9F0 as the pop pair, plus banana #FFD60A splashes. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Titan+One&family=Nunito:wght@400;700;900&display=swap — Titan One for display (it IS a sticker font), Nunito 400/700/900 for body and UI. Every card gets the die-cut treatment: 4px black border, 14px radius, 6px 6px 0 hard shadow; hero pieces get a 5px white kiss-cut border plus 3px black outline. No gradients except holographic foil badges. LAYOUT: nav as a row of rounded sticker chips; hero = tilted collage of 3-4 overlapping stickers with a fat Titan One headline slab, CTA as a pink pop-off button; product row of die-cut cards (image block, title, price, ADD sticker button); a 'sticker sheet' strip showing the whole collection; mega footer with a newsletter sticker and wobbly social chips. MOTION: stickers drop in from -60px with ±8deg rotation settling on 600ms cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) when scrolled into view; hover peels them (-2deg, scale 1.04, 180ms, shadow grows); nav chips and badges hard-invert in 0ms; decorative stickers wobble ±3deg on 5s phase-offset loops. IntersectionObserver adds .drop to reveal elements. SIGNATURE: (1) white kiss-cut die-cut borders; (2) 4px black outlines + hard shadows; (3) overlapping rotated collage; (4) foil conic-gradient badges. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML; WCAG AA — #17171B on #FFF6D6 passes hugely, keep pink/teal for accents not body text; stickers decorative layer aria-hidden; ≤1.04 hover scale to avoid layout jank; responsive: collage collapses to a 2-sticker stack under 700px, shadows shrink to 4px; reduced-motion turns drops and wobbles into simple fades.
