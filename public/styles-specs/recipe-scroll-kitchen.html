Description

A recipe that scrolls like a guided cook-along: an ingredient sidebar that highlights the ones used by the current step, doneness meters that fill as you pass checkpoints, timer chips you can start inline, and a plating finale at the bottom. For recipes, food brands, and cooking classes where the page is the sous-chef. The signature is the sync — the ingredient list always knows where you are.
