Description

A keynote stage in browser form: pure black canvas, weight-600 display type in huge sizes, and the product floating as the only lit object. Scroll drives the story — sections fade and scale cinematically as if a film editor cut them. Restraint is total: one message per viewport, nothing competing for attention. The definitive style for flagship hardware and premium product reveals.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a flagship product page in the Apple Keynote Minimal style — a cinematic black stage where the product is the only star. LOOK: pure #000000 background, raised surface #0D0D0F, text #F5F5F7, muted #86868B, hairlines #1D1D20, single accent #2997FF used ONLY for links and one CTA — never for fills. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap — Inter stands in for SF Pro: weight 600–700 display at clamp(3rem,7vw,5.5rem) with -0.02em tracking, body 17px/1.5 at weight 400. A faint radial blue glow (rgba(41,151,255,.14)) rises from the bottom of the hero. LAYOUT: slim 48px translucent nav (blur 20px, rgba(0,0,0,.7)) with tiny 12px links; full-viewport hero: headline top, product visual center (CSS-drawn device or gradient-lit panel — no external images), one sub-line, two text links ('Learn more >' style); then alternating full-viewport sections — each with exactly ONE message: a short headline + one paragraph + product visual; a spec band (4 stats, thin dividers); closing CTA; minimal footer with 12px muted links. MOTION: scroll-driven animation — product visuals scale 0.92→1 with opacity 0→1 over 100px scroll range (CSS animation-timeline: view(); JS IntersectionObserver fallback); text crossfades with 500ms opacity + 20px rise; hero ambient visual scales 1→1.06 over 20s ease-in-out alternate; spec rows stagger 80ms apart fading 400ms each. Keep timings slow and cinematic — nothing under 400ms. SIGNATURE: (1) one-message-per-viewport discipline; (2) product lit by a single rim glow on pure black; (3) blue link-only accent; (4) scroll-linked scale reveals that make the page feel edited like film. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML5; WCAG AA (#86868B on black passes 4.5:1 — verify); responsive: hero type scales via clamp, sections keep full-viewport rhythm on mobile with reduced padding; prefers-reduced-motion renders final states statically; video/poster lazy-loaded below fold; cumulative layout shift zero — reserve media aspect boxes; total JS < 8KB with CSS-only path preferred.
