Description

The aesthetics of broken video as a design system: RGB channel splits that tear headlines apart on hover, datamosh gradients that slide between keyframes, scanline crawls and corrupted-file log typography. Nothing renders cleanly — and that's the craft. For music, games and crypto projects that want digital decay as a brand asset.
