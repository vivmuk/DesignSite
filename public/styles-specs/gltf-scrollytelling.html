Description

A single GLTF object persists across the entire page while scroll rotates, explodes and reassembles it part by part, with captions pinning beside each stage. The 3D model is the storyline. For hardware explainers, automotive reveals and engineering deep-dives.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building a hardware product page in the GLTF Scrollytelling style — one persistent 3D model that scroll disassembles and reassembles like an exploded diagram. LOOK: charcoal #0F1115 background, #171A21 panels, #252A33 borders; text #EEF1F5, muted #7E8794; ember #FF6B35 highlights the active part, cool #7EC8FF tints idle parts. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Archivo:wght@600;800&family=Inter:wght@400;500&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400&display=swap (use Archivo wide-stretch if available). LAYOUT: sticky viewport: the 3D model occupies a fixed 70vh stage while content scrolls beside it. Five pinned caption stages, each 80vh of scroll: (1) overview turntable, (2) shell lifts away, (3) circuit board slides out with hotspot labels, (4) battery and sensors separate with leader lines, (5) full reassembly + CTA. Progress rail on the right shows stage 01–05. Below the scrollytelling, a calm feature grid and mega footer. MOTION: GSAP ScrollTrigger with scrub 0.5 maps scroll progress to per-part transforms (node names like 'shell', 'board', 'battery' lerp along their axis); captions cross-fade 300ms as stages change; idle turntable rotates 8°/s, pausing on pointer input; active part emissive ramps to #FF6B35 over 400ms while others dim to 60% opacity. SIGNATURE: (1) the model never leaves the screen — it IS the page; (2) exploded-view scrub synced to scroll; (3) leader-line hotspots on the active part. CONSTRAINTS: Draco-compressed GLTF under 2MB, lazy-load after first paint, cap DPR at 2, static exploded-diagram fallback for no-WebGL and reduced-motion, semantic captions with real headings, contrast at least 4.5:1.
