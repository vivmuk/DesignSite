Description

A mail client kit, designed: list-detail split with density toggle, message rows with sender avatars and unread dots, snooze and label menus that spring from row actions, a bulk-select bar that replaces the toolbar when active, and thread view with quoted-reply arrows that collapse history. For email tools, messaging, and CRMs where the inbox is the product. The signature is the select bar — bulk mode replaces context, not just adds to it.
