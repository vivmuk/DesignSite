Description

A full-bleed fragment-shader aurora — slow silk waves of teal, violet and rose — flows behind calm, well-set content that never competes with it. The background is alive at 60fps but so quiet you could read on it. For AI, SaaS and web3 brands that want atmosphere without noise.

Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Instrument+Serif&family=Outfit:wght@400;500;600&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400&display=swap — Instrument Serif for display headlines, Outfit for everything else. SIGNATURE: (1) the aurora is visible THROUGH every panel (blur refraction); (2) serif display type floating directly on the light; (3) imperceptible 30s hue drift keeping the page alive. CONSTRAINTS: shader paused when tab hidden or hero offscreen; static CSS gradient fallback for prefers-reduced-motion and no-WebGL; dither to kill banding; DPR cap 1.5; semantic HTML; body text contrast at least 4.5:1 over the darkest aurora regions.
