Description

Election night energy: county-level choropleths flipping colors as results land, tally bars racing to thresholds, too-close-to-call meters with needle dials, and anchor-desk lower-thirds for takeaways. For newsrooms, civic data, and political analysis where live tension is the design brief. The signature is the call meter — a needle that physically hovers between LEAN and TOSS-UP.
