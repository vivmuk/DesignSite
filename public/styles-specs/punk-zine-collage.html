Description

A webpage assembled like a 1982 photocopied zine: torn-paper edges, strips of tape holding sections down, ransom-note mixed type, and photocopier grain over everything. Links invert on hover like overexposed prints, margins are crooked on purpose, and red hand-scrawl annotations correct the layout. For music, activism and indie publishing with something to say.

Agent build prompt — copy this into any AI agent

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building an underground zine/music site in the Punk Zine Collage style — photocopied rage, hand-assembled, deliberately crooked but fully functional. LOOK: aged paper #F2EFE6, white paste-up surfaces #FFFFFF, near-black #1A1A1A, stencil red #D42B1F (the only color that matters), muted #6B6B60. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Special+Elite&family=Courier+Prime:wght@400;700&display=swap — Special Elite for display/headlines, Courier Prime for ALL body, lists, tables and captions. Photocopier grain sits over everything (SVG feTurbulence noise at 5% opacity, flickering). LAYOUT: nav as a strip of tape-pasted labels (each link on its own slightly-rotated paper scrap); hero headline in ransom-note style — alternating font families/sizes/weights per word, one word in red stencil; sections separated by torn-paper edges (CSS clip-path jagged polygon); photos held down with masking-tape pseudo-elements at ±4deg; a typed gig-listing table; red hand-scrawl annotations (rotated, one per section) that appear after load; stamp badges ('APPROVED BY NOBODY'); footer as the zine back page with contact/address in typewriter mono. MOTION: grain flickers opacity .04→.07 in steps(8) over 750ms infinite; tape strips lift on hover (rotate +3deg, translateY -4px, 200ms ease, shadow grows); links and stamps hard-invert (fg↔bg, 0ms transition — instant xerox flash); scrawls fade in rotated after 400ms delay when their section enters. No easing curves beyond linear/ease — this aesthetic distrusts smoothness. SIGNATURE: (1) torn edges between every section; (2) tape as the joining material; (3) ransom-note display type; (4) red scrawl annotations arguing with the layout. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML; WCAG AA — #1A1A1A on paper passes hugely; never animate full-page flicker beyond 5% opacity delta (photosensitivity safe); keep one readable hierarchy under the chaos — headings remain in DOM order; responsive: scrap rotation reduces under 700px, table becomes stacked rows; prefers-reduced-motion kills flicker and scrawl animation; no external images — CSS shapes, emoji stamps and gradient paper textures only; total JS < 6KB.
