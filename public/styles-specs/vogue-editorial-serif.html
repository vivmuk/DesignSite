Description

A runway-magazine page: towering Didone masthead, full-bleed photography bleeding to page edges, and copy that whispers in tiny letter-spaced caps. Every element is aligned to a strict editorial grid so the photography carries the drama. Used when the brand itself is the story and restraint reads as luxury.
