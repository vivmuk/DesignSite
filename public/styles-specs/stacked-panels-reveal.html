Description

Sections that stack like cards being dealt: each panel pins to the top, the previous one dims and scales back 4%, and a corner tab marks the handoff. For product tours, process explainers, and case studies where each step deserves its own full-screen moment. The signature is the handoff tab — a corner label that flips as the next panel takes over.
