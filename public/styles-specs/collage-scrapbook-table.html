Description

A desk covered in a life: taped polaroids at angles, washi tape strips in three patterns, ticket stubs, handwritten captions, and doodle arrows connecting related things. For travelogues, personal blogs, and gift sites where warmth beats polish. The signature is the tape — every element is held down by washi, and hover peels it slightly.
