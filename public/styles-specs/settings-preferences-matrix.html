Description

The settings page treated as a designed product: left section nav with active rails, toggle rows with descriptive sublabels, select and slider controls aligned to one grid, a dirty-state banner when changes are unsaved, and a danger zone that looks genuinely dangerous. For app settings, admin panels, and preference centers where this exact screen decides whether users feel in control. The signature is the dirty banner — unsaved changes make themselves known politely.
