Description

Riso print energy on screen: two fluorescent inks (pink and blue) overlapping with deliberate misregistration, halftone dot texture, grain, and stapled-zine layout chaos held together by strong columns. For zines, indie music, and art collectives that want print soul with web motion. The signature is the misregistration — headings printed twice, offset, in both inks.
