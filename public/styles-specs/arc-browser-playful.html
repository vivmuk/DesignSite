Description

Arc Browser's design language distilled: huge 24px-radius surfaces in saturated paint-chip gradients, oversized rounded type, and micro-interactions that behave like physical objects — springs everywhere, nothing snaps. Whimsy is systematic, not random: every color comes from the same candy palette. Built for consumer apps and creative tools that want joy without losing polish.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building the landing page for a consumer app in the Arc Browser Playful style — joyful, physical, springy, immaculately polished. LOOK: deep plum #1A1423 base, raised surfaces #261D33, text #FFF4F9 with muted #B8A8C8, borders #3A2C4E. The signature palette is the paint-chip gradient: linear-gradient(135deg,#FF6B9D 0%,#C084FC 45%,#7EE8FA 100%) used on hero panels, buttons and orbs. Import fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bricolage+Grotesque:opsz,wght@12..96,400;12..96,600;12..96,800&family=Nunito+Sans:wght@400;600;700&display=swap — Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display (clamp 2.8–5rem, -0.02em), Nunito Sans for body. All radii are oversized: 24px panels, 999px pills. LAYOUT: floating pill nav (centered, backdrop-blur, 999px radius) with logo, links and gradient CTA; hero is a giant 24px-radius gradient panel containing headline + subcopy + dual CTAs, with two smaller tilted panels peeking behind; a 3-card feature row of tilted candy surfaces; an onboarding-steps strip (3 numbered steps with spring-in); playful mega footer. MOTION: everything springs, nothing snaps — hover pop scale 1.03 and press squash (scaleX 1.06/scaleY .94) over 120ms cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1); hero panel enters 0.6→1.04→0.98→1 over 700ms; a 300px gradient orb lags the cursor at lerp 0.08/frame with mix-blend-mode:screen; emoji decorations wiggle ±8deg 1.6s infinite alternate; primary CTA click fires a 24-particle CSS confetti burst (transform-based, cleaned up after 1.2s). Vanilla JS only. SIGNATURE: (1) paint-chip gradient as the identity surface; (2) chewy spring button physics on every interactive element; (3) cursor-chasing light orb; (4) oversized 24px radii everywhere — audit and reject any small radius. CONSTRAINTS: semantic HTML5; WCAG AA — verify #B8A8C8 on #1A1423; responsive: hero panel text scales down fluidly, grid single column under 700px, orb hidden on touch devices; prefers-reduced-motion disables springs, orb and wiggle; keyboard focus rings visible (2px #7EE8FA offset); no images — gradients and emoji only; total JS < 12KB.
