Description

The sticker album as interface: a grid of numbered slots, shiny foil rarities, checklist progress, swap badges, and a packet-opening reveal animation for new content. For communities, collectibles, and sports fans where completion is the game. The signature is the packet tear — new items arrive by ripping open a foil packet.
