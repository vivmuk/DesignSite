Description

A New York Times-style scrollytelling layout: a visual stage stays pinned beside copy that scrolls past, stepping charts and diagrams through annotated states. Warm paper, editorial serifs and hairline column rules keep it feeling like journalism rather than a product page. Use it when data and narrative must build an argument together.
