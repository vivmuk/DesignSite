Description

A full-bleed GPU fluid simulation floods the hero — the cursor drags luminous ink through it while headline and CTA float above the chaos. Below the fold, calm editorial sections recover composure. For agencies, AI products and events that want the wow to be physical.

ROLE: You are a senior front-end engineer building an agency/AI-product landing page in the WebGL Fluid Hero style — a cursor-stirred GPU fluid simulation IS the hero. LOOK: near-black #04040A base with a #141031 gradient depth; surfaces #0D0D18, borders #1E1E33; text #F2F2FA, muted #8B8BA6; dye colors are luminous cyan #7DF9FF and violet #B26CFF injected into a dark fluid field. Import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Clash+Display:wght@600;700&family=Satoshi:wght@400;500&display=swap (use fontshare/fallbacks if needed — Space Grotesk + Inter acceptable substitutes). Headline floats ABOVE the canvas at pointer-opposed parallax (0.02 factor, 400ms lerp). LAYOUT: transparent nav over the sim; full-viewport hero canvas with centered headline + single ghost CTA; below the fold, deliberately calm editorial: feature grid on #0D0D18, an about section, a contact form-stack, mega footer. The contrast between chaotic hero and composed body is the concept. MOTION: WebGL2 fluid simulation — pointer velocity injects force + dye each frame, dissipation 0.98, curl 12, 60fps target with devicePixelRatio capped at 1.5; idle 4s triggers an autonomous center plume; body sections fade-up 24px staggered 100ms via IntersectionObserver. SIGNATURE: (1) the sim reacts to every pointer move — no dead pixels in the hero; (2) headline counter-parallax against the fluid; (3) a small fps/quality toggle chip in the hero corner. CONSTRAINTS: pause the sim when hero is offscreen or tab hidden; provide a static gradient poster + no-sim fallback for reduced-motion and WebGL failure; semantic HTML; body copy contrast at least 4.5:1; total JS for sim under 40KB.
