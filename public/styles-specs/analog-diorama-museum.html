Description

Museum diorama cases as layout: layered shadowbox scenes with real depth between planes, brass plaque labels with engraved type, spotlight cones picking out artifacts, and velvet-rope section dividers. For museums, exhibitions, and cultural archives that want physical curation on the web. The signature is the spotlight — each artifact sits in its own cone of light.
