Description

The quiet luxury movement as interface: vast whitespace, type set at 300 weight, bone and taupe cashmere tones, no logo wall, tiny serif moments, and CTAs that whisper rather than shout. For fashion houses, skincare, and interior studios where confidence is the absence of effort. The signature is the whisper CTA — letterspaced small caps that breathe.
