Description

Soviet constructivist posters turned into interfaces: strong diagonals, red-black-cream, photo blocks cut at angles, and slab condensed type shouting in caps. Built for campaigns, activist orgs, and cultural statements that want the page to take a side. The signature is the wedge — accent shapes that literally point at the message.
