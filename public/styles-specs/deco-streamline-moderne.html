Description

Late deco streamlined like a 1936 locomotive: chrome horizontal bands, porthole windows as image frames, muted teal and cream, speed lines streaking behind headlines, geometric sans with wide tracking. For heritage hotels, classic motoring brands, diners, and any product that wants vintage motion without camp. The signature is the porthole — circular frames everywhere the eye lands.
