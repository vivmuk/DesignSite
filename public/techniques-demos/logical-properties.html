Logical Properties

One stylesheet, two directions. Switching dir flips margins, borders, floats — no RTL overrides anywhere.

Flow-relative layout

Every dimension on this page uses logical properties: margin-inline-start, padding-block, border-inline-start, inline-size. When the document direction flips to RTL, the browser maps start/end to the new physical edges automatically.
