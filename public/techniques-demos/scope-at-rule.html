Scoped Styles: @scope

Both widgets use the exact same .title and .body classes — @scope keeps their styles encapsulated without renaming anything.

@scope isn't supported in this browser — both widgets will share the last-declared styles.

Widget A title

Styled by the widget-a scope block. Violet accent, italic body. The :scope pseudo-class targets the scoping root itself, giving this card a violet border.

Widget B title

Styled by the widget-b scope block. Green, uppercase micro-title. Identical class names — zero collisions, zero BEM prefixes.

Scope also solves the proximity problem: a donut like @scope (.article) to (.advert) styles descendants but stops at a boundary element — something descendant selectors cannot express.
