The Vertical Web

Long before screens, book designers solved vertical rhythm. This page borrows their grammar: a spine that reads top-to-bottom, a pull-quote turned ninety degrees, and a bilingual body where horizontal Latin meets vertical Japanese without a seam.

Text flows the way the page breathes.

縦書きの美しさは、余白と流れの調和にある。横書きと縦書きが一つのページで共存するとき、文字は建築になる。

Tune the spine with writing-mode: vertical-rl, rotate a quote with text-orientation: sideways, and let CJK stay upright while Latin rotates — one property, three behaviors.
