Chapter 01 — Origin

The pane that never moves

The left half is position:sticky with height:100vh; the right half scrolls independently. This is the whole trick — no scroll-jacking, no JS animation loop driving the layout.

Agency sites love this pattern because the visual half can hold attention while the narrative half earns the scroll.

Chapter 02 — Exchange

Scenes swap at boundaries

An IntersectionObserver on each section swaps the pinned canvas scene when a section crosses the middle of the viewport. The image cross-fades; the text never waits for it.

Chapter 03 — Collapse

One file, two behaviours

On narrow viewports this collapses to a single column and the sticky pane becomes a simple header. Pure flexbox plus one media query.

Chapter 04 — Return

Scroll progress, subtly shown

The thin bar at the bottom-center tracks the right pane's scroll. Note the left canvas hue shifts with the active chapter.
