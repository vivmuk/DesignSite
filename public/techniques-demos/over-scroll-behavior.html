overscroll-behavior & Scroll Containment

Open the modal, scroll its inner panel to the end, and keep scrolling: with overscroll-behavior: contain the page behind stays still.

Scroll down this long page first so there is scroll momentum available. Then open the modal above. Inside the modal is an inner scrollable panel with forty items.

When the inner panel's overscroll-behavior is auto, hitting its scroll end chains the scroll to the page behind — the background moves while you think you are scrolling the panel. With contain, the scroll stops at the boundary; with none, even rubber-banding bounce is suppressed.

This is critical for modals, drawers, chat logs, and embedded feeds — anywhere nested scrolling tricks users. Scroll chaining was default browser behavior since forever; overscroll-behavior finally lets you cut the chain declaratively, one line of CSS, no scroll-lock JavaScript hacks like body position fixed locks.
